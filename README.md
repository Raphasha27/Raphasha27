I am **Koketso Raphasha**, a **Systems Architect, AI Engineer, and co-founder of Kirov Dynamics** based in **Johannesburg, South Africa**.

My work sits at the intersection of:

- Sovereign AI infrastructure
- digital trust systems
- distributed architecture
- performance-focused engineering
- human-centered AI interaction

I focus on turning complex technical strategy into production-shaped systems that are secure, explainable, and built for real social and economic impact.
