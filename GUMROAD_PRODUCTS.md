# Gumroad Products Guide

## How to Publish Products on Gumroad

1. Go to https://app.gumroad.com and log in with your account
2. Click "New Product" in the top right
3. Fill in the product details from the templates below
4. Set pricing and publish

## Product Improvement Tips

1. **Storefront** — After publishing at least one product, your storefront at raphashakoketso.gumroad.com will go live
2. **Rich content** — Add screenshots and demo videos to each product
3. **SEO** — Use detailed descriptions with keywords for search visibility
4. **License** — Set clear usage terms (personal use, commercial use)
5. **Updates** — Push updates regularly to maintain customer trust
